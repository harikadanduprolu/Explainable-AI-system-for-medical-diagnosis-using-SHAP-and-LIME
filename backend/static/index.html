About Explainable Medical AI

🎯 Purpose

This system provides AI-powered disease risk predictions with transparent, explainable reasoning using SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) and LIME (Local Interpretable Model-agnostic Explanations).

🏥 Diseases Assessed

  • Sepsis - Life-threatening response to infection
  • Kidney Failure - Acute or chronic kidney dysfunction
  • Heart Disease - Cardiovascular conditions
  • Diabetes - Blood glucose regulation disorders
  • Anemia - Low hemoglobin/red blood cells
  • Mortality Risk - Overall survival prediction

🔬 Technology

  • Backend: FastAPI (Python)
  • ML Models: XGBoost, LightGBM, Random Forest Ensembles
  • Explainability: SHAP values for feature importance
  • Data: Trained on MIMIC-III medical dataset

⚠️ Disclaimer

This system is for research and educational purposes only. It should NOT be used for actual medical diagnosis or treatment decisions. Always consult qualified healthcare professionals for medical advice.
